Manage Anki material for the current Roman Letter.

IMPORTANT: Do not simply list words to memorize — generate actual Anki-ready cards.

There are TWO card types:

### TYPE 1 — VOCABULARY
FRONT: English word/expression
BACK: Portuguese meaning / Part of speech / Short natural example / Register/use note

### TYPE 2 — SENTENCE
FRONT: Portuguese sentence
BACK: Natural English sentence / Key expression/grammar / Short explanation

Sentence cards are extremely important — the goal is learning English in context, not memorizing isolated words.

Only include high-value cards. Do not create duplicate cards for words/sentences already listed in `ROMAN_LETTERS_ENGLISH_TRACKER.md`.

At the end, provide a clean "COPY FOR ANKI" section using TAB-separated values for bulk import: `Front<TAB>Back`. Do not put extra tabs inside individual fields.

Also report: how many new vocabulary cards were created; how many new sentence cards were created; how many potential duplicates were skipped.

Update the cumulative Anki count and card list in `ROMAN_LETTERS_ENGLISH_TRACKER.md`.
