description: Speaking practice based on the current Roman Letter
---

Create a speaking practice session based on the current Roman Letter.

Give prompts rather than a memorized script. Start easy and gradually become harder. For example:
1. Who wrote the letter?
2. What happened?
3. Why did the writer contact the recipient?
4. What did the writer want?
5. What do you think about the writer's attitude?

Encourage answering using the target vocabulary and grammar from the letter.
